## 👋 About Me

I'm a **Big Data Analytics student** who enjoys the space where **software development and data meet**. My main path runs through **C#/.NET, Python, and SQL**, and right now I'm focused on:

- 🧱 Strengthening my **backend development** fundamentals
- 📊 Building **data-driven applications** instead of just notebooks
- 🔁 Turning what I learn into projects I can actually **build, test, and improve**

This profile isn't a finished portfolio. It's a living record of what I'm learning, building, and improving over time.
